# Capture protocol (part of the product)

Good footage is what makes tracking reliable. Show this card to every master and learner.

1. **Steady phone.** Tripod, clamp, or propped against something. Don't film handheld.
2. **Frame the work, not the person.** Hands and parts fill the frame. Faces are hidden anyway, but keep them out when you can.
3. **Lay parts out first.** Put tools and new parts in view (tray, cloth, newspaper) before you start.
4. **Pause after each step.** Take your hands away for about one second. That pause is how USTAAD finds step boundaries.
5. **Light.** Daylight or a bright lamp from the side. Avoid strong backlight (windows behind the work).
6. **Same angle for learners.** Film your attempt from roughly where the master's camera was.
7. **Landscape, 1080p is plenty.** Any phone format works; USTAAD converts it.
