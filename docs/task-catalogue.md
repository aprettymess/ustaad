# USTAAD task catalogue: jobs you can film at home

Every task here is safe, common, and **camera-checkable**. Each one has visually distinct parts (good for SAM),
at least one real "must come first" step (so order mistakes can be caught), and natural mistakes to seed.
Excluded on purpose: mains electricity (bulbs, fans, wiring), gas (cylinders, stove burners), UPS/battery
terminals, medical, anything that needs a certification.

Rating key: **SAM** = how easy the parts are to track · **Order** = strength of a real precondition ·
**PII** = private things that naturally appear (good for showing redaction) · **Reps** = how easy it is to
repeat for the benchmark.

## Recommended line-up
| Role | Task | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Hero (dad)** | Car tyre change | Big parts, classic order mistake, plate + house number naturally in shot |
| **Bike hero / second domain** | Bicycle puncture repair *or* motorcycle spark-plug check | Very common in Pakistan; proves it generalises beyond cars |
| **Benchmark (repeat 8×)** | Bicycle chain clean & lube | 2-minute task, distinct bottles = perfect "wrong part" error, repeatable all evening |
| **Backup benchmark** | Engine-oil dipstick check | 1-minute task, built-in order rule (wipe before reading) |

## Bikes and motorcycles

### 1. Bicycle chain clean & lube (BEST BENCHMARK)
- **Parts (SAM concepts):** bicycle chain, rag, degreaser bottle, brush, lube bottle
- **Steps:** rag under the chain → spray degreaser → scrub with brush → wipe chain with rag → apply lube → wipe excess
- **Must come first:** degrease *before* lube; scrub *before* wipe
- **Seeded mistakes:** lube before degreaser (order) · skip the wipe (skipped) · pick up the degreaser instead of the lube (wrong part: look-alike bottles) · spray the tyre (extra)
- **Filming:** side-on, phone on a stool at chain height, bike on its stand or upside down · **SAM** ★★★ · **Order** ★★★ · **PII** ★ · **Reps** ★★★

### 2. Bicycle puncture repair (inner tube patch)
- **Parts:** wheel, tyre lever, inner tube, bucket of water, patch kit (sandpaper, glue, patch), pump
- **Steps:** remove wheel → lever tyre off → pull tube out → inflate a little and dunk in water to find the leak → mark it → sand → glue → patch → tube back in → tyre back on → pump up
- **Must come first:** find leak before patching · sand before glue · tube inside tyre before pumping
- **Seeded mistakes:** patch without sanding (skipped) · pump before seating the tyre (order) · wrong patch size (wrong part)
- **Caveat:** glue drying time is a "master's tip — not checked" · **SAM** ★★ (tube is floppy) · **Order** ★★★ · **PII** ★★ (street, neighbours) · **Reps** ★

### 3. Motorcycle spark plug check / clean (CD70 / 125)
- **Parts:** spark plug cap, spark plug, plug spanner, wire brush or sandpaper, feeler gauge (optional)
- **Steps:** engine cold and off → pull the plug cap → unscrew the plug → inspect/clean the tip → (check gap) → screw back by hand first → tighten with spanner → refit the cap
- **Must come first:** cap off before spanner · hand-thread before spanner (thread safety)
- **Seeded mistakes:** spanner before removing cap (order) · forget to refit cap (skipped)
- **Caveat:** "tighten a quarter turn past snug" = tip, not checked. Engine must be cold · **SAM** ★★ (small parts; film close) · **Order** ★★★ · **PII** ★★★ (number plate!) · **Reps** ★★

### 4. Motorcycle chain lube / tension check
- **Parts:** rear wheel, chain, lube can, rag, axle nut, spanner
- **Steps:** bike on main stand → wipe chain → lube while turning the wheel → wipe excess → (check slack with a finger: tip)
- **Must come first:** wipe before lube; main stand before turning the wheel · **SAM** ★★ · **Order** ★★ · **PII** ★★★ · **Reps** ★★

### 5. Bicycle brake pad replacement (rim brakes)
- **Parts:** brake pad, brake arm, Allen key, wheel · **Must come first:** release the brake before removing the pad · **SAM** ★★ · **Order** ★★ · **Reps** ★★

## Car (no tools beyond the car kit)

### 6. Car tyre change (HERO)
- **Parts:** wheel, lug nut, jack, lug wrench, spare tyre
- **Steps:** loosen nuts (on the ground) → place jack → lift → remove nuts → wheel off → spare on → nuts by hand → lower → tighten fully
- **Must come first:** **loosen nuts before jacking** (classic) · lower before final tightening
- **Seeded mistakes:** jack first (order) · forget a nut (wrong part / skipped)
- **SAM** ★★★ · **Order** ★★★ · **PII** ★★★ (plate, house number, neighbours) · **Reps** ★

### 7. Engine-oil dipstick check (BACKUP BENCHMARK)
- **Parts:** dipstick, rag, oil filler cap, oil bottle (if topping up)
- **Steps:** engine off, car level → pull dipstick → wipe on rag → re-insert fully → pull again → read → re-insert → (top up through filler cap)
- **Must come first:** **wipe before reading**; filler cap off before pouring
- **Seeded mistakes:** read without wiping (order/skipped) · pour into the dipstick tube (wrong part)
- **SAM** ★★ · **Order** ★★★ · **PII** ★ · **Reps** ★★★

### 8. Wiper blade replacement
- **Parts:** wiper arm, old blade, new blade · **Must come first:** lift the arm before unclipping · **SAM** ★★★ · **Order** ★★ · **PII** ★★ · **Reps** ★★★

## Inside the house

### 9. Split-AC filter cleaning
- **Parts:** front panel, air filter, brush, bucket or sink
- **Steps:** AC off at the remote → open front panel → slide filters out → wash/brush → dry → slide back in → close panel
- **Must come first:** filters back in before closing the panel; AC off first
- **Seeded mistakes:** close panel without filters (skipped) · put a filter in upside down (wrong part/orientation, partially checkable)
- **SAM** ★★★ · **Order** ★★ · **PII** ★★★ (room, family photos, calendars) · **Reps** ★★

### 10. Sink P-trap unclog
- **Parts:** bucket, P-trap, slip nut, brush, rubber gloves
- **Steps:** bucket under the trap → unscrew slip nuts → remove trap → clean → refit → hand-tighten → run water to check
- **Must come first:** **bucket before unscrewing** (very visual)
- **Seeded mistakes:** unscrew without bucket (order) · forget a washer (skipped) · **SAM** ★★ · **Order** ★★★ · **PII** ★ · **Reps** ★★★

### 11. Tap aerator cleaning
- **Parts:** tap, aerator, cloth, toothbrush, bowl of vinegar
- **Steps:** cloth around aerator → unscrew → soak/brush → screw back · **Must come first:** cloth before pliers (protect finish) · **SAM** ★★ (small) · **Order** ★★ · **Reps** ★★★

### 12. Washing machine lint / drain filter
- **Parts:** filter cover, filter, towel, shallow tray
- **Steps:** machine off → towel + tray down → open cover → unscrew filter slowly → clean → refit → close cover
- **Must come first:** tray before opening the filter · **SAM** ★★ · **Order** ★★★ · **PII** ★★ · **Reps** ★★

### 13. Water-filter cartridge change (under-sink / RO)
- **Parts:** filter housing, cartridge, housing wrench, O-ring, bucket
- **Steps:** water off → release pressure → bucket under → unscrew housing → swap cartridge → check O-ring → refit
- **Must come first:** water off and pressure released before unscrewing · **SAM** ★★ · **Order** ★★★ · **Reps** ★★

## Desk tasks (best for controlled benchmarks under a top-down phone)

### 14. PC RAM + M.2 SSD install (on a spare board or an open case)
- **Parts:** RAM stick, M.2 SSD, M.2 screw, screwdriver, motherboard
- **Must come first:** remove the M.2 heatsink before inserting the SSD; open the RAM latches first
- **Seeded mistakes:** RAM in the wrong slot pair (wrong part, if slots are visible) · skip the M.2 screw (skipped)
- **SAM** ★★ · **Order** ★★ · **PII** ★ · **Reps** ★★★ · Unpowered, with anti-static care

### 15. Laptop fan dust cleaning (bottom panel off)
- **Parts:** bottom panel, screws, screwdriver, fan, brush, air blower
- **Must come first:** shut down + unplug before screws; hold the fan still before blowing air · **SAM** ★★ · **Order** ★★ · **Reps** ★★

3. Motorcycle at home? → film the **spark-plug check** as the "works for bikes too" clip in the video's vision section.
